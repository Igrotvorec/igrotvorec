# Russian bank superapp mini-games: source-checked case notes

Research cut-off and access date: **2026-10-18**. Scope: T-Bank «Ряд наград», T-Bank «Магия отпуска», SberСпасибо «Ловлю на слове», Alfa «Алхимия акций». Historical campaign rules are not represented as current availability.

## Evidence rules and classification

- **Bank primary** means a bank-owned page or a publication by the bank’s own named account. **Creator primary** means the project team/studio’s own account of its work, including a case hosted on a portfolio platform. Both are self-reports, not independently audited analytics.
- **Secondary press / editorial** is labeled explicitly, even where the writer quotes a bank representative. An agency publishing an article about another company’s game is not automatically that game’s creator.
- Each substantive claim below links to its source ID. Exact quotations retain the source’s wording; short excerpts are deliberately used instead of reproducing entire articles. Source titles, publication dates, publishers and URLs are in the source register.
- Reported totals do not establish incremental customer acquisition, increased banking activity, loyalty uplift or causation. No prize budget, prize count, level count, development duration or planned target is treated as observed engagement.
- Genre values use only `arcade / match3 / runner / quiz / clicker / puzzle / other`. Audience values use only `seniors / kids / teens / adults / general`. **All four receive `general`**: this is a conservative classification, not a measured demographic claim. None of the checked game-specific rules establishes a demographic segment suitable for a narrower label. Banking/investment context alone is not evidence for an adults-only audience.

## 1. T-Bank / Tinkoff — «Ряд наград»

### Identity, access, period and audience

- **Bank primary:** the current bank announcement says the game became available in the bank app on **2023-09-04**, through search or the «Город» section. It explicitly states availability **until 2023-10-01**; the grand-prize drawing was scheduled for **2023-10-12**. These are campaign/access dates, not a published analytics window. [R1]
- Exact timing excerpt: «Головоломка будет доступна в приложении до 1 октября 2023 года, главные призы разыграем 12 октября.» [R1]
- Classification: **`match3`, `general`**. The bank itself names the match-three genre. No game-specific minimum/maximum age or measured audience distribution was established from the checked announcement. `general` must not be read as a claim that minors were eligible. [R1]

### Mechanics — bank-confirmed

| Claim | Exact source wording / evidence | Interpretation and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Swap tiles to match at least three horizontally or vertically | «Их нужно менять местами, чтобы собрать минимум три одинаковых элемента в ряд по горизонтали или по вертикали.» [R1] | A match-three puzzle, not a quiz or clicker. |
| Fixed moves, no timer | «В каждом раунде у вас есть двадцать ходов. Ограничения по времени нет» [R1] | Twenty moves per round; not a speed-based round. |
| Five starting lives; a round consumes one; replenishment is per life | «За каждый раунд сгорает одна жизнь, она восстановится через 12 часов.» [R1] | The same section specifies five starting lives for five rounds. Do not replace this with a generic “12–24 hours” rule or claim all five restore every 12 hours. |
| Product/content tasks feed an in-game currency economy | Bank lists watching five app stories, enabling investment-savings rounding, inviting a friend and donating to charity; coins buy lives and boosters. [R1] | Documented incentive design, not measured completion, conversion or charitable participation. |
| Weekly competition across five leagues | Bank describes five leagues; top five move up, bottom five move down, with weekly movement possible. [R1] | A recurrence/competition mechanism, not a measured weekly return rate. |
| Rewards are linked to progress and rank | Bank describes cashback, partner discounts, prize-draw tickets and guaranteed prizes for the top five in each league. [R1] | Reward inventory and prize values do not measure engagement. |

## 2. T-Bank / Tinkoff — «Магия отпуска»

### Identity, provenance, access and period

- **Bank primary:** game availability is explicitly **2024-07-10 through 2024-08-27**, via app search or «Город» → «Путешествия» → «Магия отпуска». The bank announcement’s structured publication date is 2024-07-16, not the game’s start date. [V1]
- The current announcement says the game ended and winners were selected on **2024-08-27**. **Prize-draw ticket earning had a separate cutoff, 2024-08-12 inclusive.** Do not substitute the ticket cutoff for the game’s stated availability period. [V1]
- **Firsthand creator account:** Ксения Тяжельникова describes the team’s decisions, credits collaborators and refers to the T-Bank special-projects team. Her exact statement is «Мы создали игру-раннер, где клиент проходит уровни и зарабатывает монетки.» Her formal role and any external implementation studio are not identified in that post. [V2]
- Classification: **`runner`, `general`**. Runner is the team participant’s explicit genre, corroborated by the bank’s obstacle/coin mechanics. Neither checked account supplies game-specific age eligibility or a measured demographic breakdown. [V1][V2]

### Engagement results: explicit absence in checked firsthand sources

**No numerical player count, games/rounds, time spent, return rate, retention, target-action conversion or incremental travel-product uplift is reported in V1 or V2.** The creator post is detailed about design and production, but not about measured outcomes. [V1][V2]

The following are **not engagement metrics**:

- **24 levels / four locations:** content scope. [V1][V2]
- **27 tasks / 34 bonuses / 17 segmentations:** configuration and reward inventory in the creator account. [V2]
- **Nine months of work / eight business lines / seven studies:** production or organizational scope; study samples/results not provided. [V2]
- **A maximum of 20 draw tickets per player / draw prizes and their budgets:** rules and incentives; not tickets actually earned, players or retention. [V1]

A Cookies Games retrospective and a Gamification Now editorial review were also checked; neither supplies measured engagement outcomes or establishes that its publisher developed this game. Do not turn their claims about “attracting users” or “motivating return” into quantified results. [V3][V4]

**Reusable formulation:** “T-Bank and a participating team member document a 24-level runner with travel-product tasks, boosters, a coin shop and daily rewards; no numerical engagement outcome was located in those accounts.” [V1][V2]

## 3. SberСпасибо — «Ловлю на слове»

### Identity, provenance, period and audience

- **Secondary press, brand-sourced:** Sostav’s 2023-02-05 case names Дмитрий Завиша, director of product at SberСпасибо, as the person who explained the game’s goals, mechanics and results. The article lists a SberСпасибо project team. It is not a bank-owned page or an independent analytics audit. [S1]
- Sostav states the launch month was **August 2022** and reports outcomes **“за шесть месяцев”**. Exact start/end dates of that measurement interval are not supplied; do not manufacture an August-to-February cohort cutoff. [S1]
- **Creator corroboration, but not primary numerical corroboration:** Rev.Games’ own homepage includes «Ловлю на Слове» and «Игра для „Спасибо“ СберБанка» in its cases. Gamification Now explicitly says «Решение разработано командой Rev.games». Together these support Rev.Games’ project association and the reported development credit; sole authorship and the division of work with the bank are not established. [S2][S3]
- Classification: **`puzzle`, `general`**. Word deduction is not a knowledge quiz. Registered SberСпасибо members could enter via the loyalty website/app, through «Спасибомания». The checked case does not give game-specific age conditions or measured demographics. [S1]

The CEO’s DTF account repeats the project account and metrics with a displayed publication date of **2025-11-11**. - The bank discusses the broader transition to in-house Alfa Games, but does not provide this project’s full production credits. REB8T explicitly presents the game as its work. These sources do not establish who owned every component; do not erase the creator attribution by assuming the whole game was made only in-house. [A1][A2][A3]

The bank describes users of different ages, incomes and investing experience. The agency’s “14 to 65” observation concerns **Alfa games generally**, not the measured audience or legal eligibility of this game; it does not justify separate `teens` or `seniors` labels here.
